Enterprise Architecture

The 4 Pillars of EUDR Automation

Purpose-built to address every technical and legal mandate of Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 without manual intervention.

📐

1. Geodesic Spatial Engine

Enforces the strict 4-hectare polygon rule (Art. 9). Computes exact ellipsoidal area via WGS84 Geod and automatically self-heals unclosed or overlapping GIS boundaries.

🛰️

2. Deforestation Radar

Overlays Copernicus Sentinel-2 NDVI, Hansen Global Forest Change, and JRC Forest Cover against the strict 31 December 2020 cut-off date with sub-pixel sensitivity.

⚖️

3. Legal Document Auditor

Validates national production legality, customary land titles, harvest permits, and FPIC (Free, Prior and Informed Consent) based on country risk tiers.

🔒

4. TRACES-NT Direct DDS

Generates standardized Due Diligence Statements (DDS) with HMAC-SHA256 signatures, verifiable audit trails, and automated B2G API submission to EU Customs.
