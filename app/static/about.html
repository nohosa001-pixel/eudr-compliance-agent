About EUDRAgent Platform

Built for global commodity traders, European importers, and farming cooperatives to eliminate EUDR compliance complexity with automated satellite audit verification.

Copernicus Sentinel-2 Live

Multi-spectral satellite radar imagery (B4/B8 NDVI) at 10m resolution, cross-referenced with JRC Global Forest Cover and Hansen Global Forest Change datasets.

Cryptographic Audit Chain

Every Due Diligence Statement (DDS) is signed with an immutable SHA-256 digital hash, compliant with EU Regulation 2023/1115 Art. 9, 10, and 12.

Sub-Second Verification

Processes complex multi-polygon farm geometries (WGS84 EPSG:4326), performing 4ha area checks and topological validation in under 200 milliseconds.
